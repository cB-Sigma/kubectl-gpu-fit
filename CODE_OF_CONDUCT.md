# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and considerate, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work and not the person, and help make this a project that people of any background and experience level are comfortable contributing to. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language or imagery are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other project space.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant linked above applies to everyone taking part in this project, including maintainers.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately through the contact details on the repository owner's GitHub profile ([@cb-Sigma](https://github.com/cb-Sigma)). Please do not raise conduct concerns in public issues.

Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will review every report, may ask for more information, and will take the action they consider appropriate, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
